# Changelog

All notable changes to the ElevenLabs integration for Gladys Assistant.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- First version: ElevenLabs becomes a voice of Gladys (Gladys 5.3 or later).
  Select it on the Artificial Intelligence page, and the "Talk on a speaker"
  scene action speaks with the ElevenLabs voice of your choice. The
  administrator enters the API key, the voice (name or id, with a "List my
  voices" button), the model, the speed and the stability. A sentence already
  spoken is reused without using credits, and the connection status shows the
  credits left.
